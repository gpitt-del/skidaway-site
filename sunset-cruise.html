Sunset on Moon River

A private evening on the water, timed to the sunset and home before dark.

The evening

We leave Delegal Creek Marina a little over two hours before sunset and take our time through the marsh, where the dolphins usually are. Then we settle in on Moon River, or out on Ossabaw Sound when the wind and tide are right, and watch the sun go down. We head in with the last light and are tied up before dark.

Good to know

Departure time moves with the season; we confirm it when you book. Bring your own cocktails or a snack if you like; there is a table aboard. A light layer is good for the ride home.

If the captain cancels for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date. More in the FAQ.
