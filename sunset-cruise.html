Sunset cruise · Savannah, Georgia

Sunset Cruise

Golden light on the marsh, the day’s last dolphins, and the sun going down over the sound. Bring the cocktails.

The evening

We leave Delegal Creek Marina about an hour and a half before sunset and take our time through the marsh, where the dolphins usually are. Then we settle in on Moon River, or out on Ossabaw Sound when the wind and tide are right, and watch the sun go down. We head in with the last light and are tied up before dark.

Good to know

Departure time moves with the season; we confirm it when you book. Bring a light layer for the ride home, and your own cocktails or a snack if you like; there is a table aboard.
