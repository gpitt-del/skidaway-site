Half day · about 4 hours
Islands and Empty Beaches

Barrier islands and miles of sand with nobody on them, with time on the beach when the tide and the day allow. Dolphins often alongside, shorebirds working the bars, the open sound ahead. The right half day for a girls’ weekend, or just to get out and take in what Savannah and the Lowcountry have to offer.
