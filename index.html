Our introduction to the Low Country

The Moon River Cruise

From Delegal Creek to Moon River and back, with a third-generation Savannahian at the helm. Along the way: Civil War earthworks hidden in the islands, when the tide lets us in close, and traces of people who lived on these shores around the time the pyramids went up. Dolphins are regulars. Keep an eye out for bald eagles, sea turtles, leaping rays and tarpon.

About 90 minutes · $425 for the whole boat
Bring a picnic or an early dinner; there is a table aboard. Want longer? Add time in half-hour steps.

How it works

Hold your date

A $100 deposit reserves the boat. It is refundable if you cancel 30 or more days out. You can pay the rest at the dock, by card or cash.

Before you come

Not a Landings resident? We arrange the gate pass; we just need the driver's name. Bring hats and sun shirts. Mineral lotion is welcome aboard; spray sunscreen is not.

If the weather turns

The captain makes the call. If he cancels for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.
