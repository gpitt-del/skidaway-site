Cruises · about an hour and a half

The Lowcountry, from the water.

The Moon River Cruise

Cruise through the wispy, verdant marsh, past Civil War relics, to the namesake of the song Savannah’s Johnny Mercer made famous. Dolphins, bald eagles, osprey and pelicans keep you company.

About 1.5 hours

Sunset Cruise

Golden light on the marsh, the day’s last dolphins, and the sun going down over the sound. Bring the cocktails.

About 1.5 hours
