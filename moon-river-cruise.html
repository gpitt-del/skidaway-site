Our introduction to the Low Country

The Moon River Cruise

Delegal Creek to Moon River and back. About ninety minutes on the water, and about four thousand years of history along the way.

What you will see

Dolphins, and whatever else turns up

Dolphins are regulars. Bald eagles, sea turtles, leaping rays, tarpon and the occasional shark are all seen from the boat.

A Civil War secret

When the tide is high enough to get in close, we pass earthworks and batteries that once guarded the back door to Savannah. Most people who live here have never laid eyes on them.

Four thousand years of neighbors

People were building on these shores around the time the pyramids went up. We will show you where, from the water, and leave everything as we found it.

Moon River itself

The river Johnny Mercer knew as a boy, later renamed for his song. It is quieter than you would expect from something that famous.

Aboard

Your captain is a third-generation Savannahian and a third-generation Landings resident, so the stories come with the trip. The boat has shade, a table and a private head. Bring a picnic, or make it an early dinner on the water.
