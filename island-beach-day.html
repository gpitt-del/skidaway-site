Island Beach Day

A private beach on Wassaw or Ossabaw, shells and sea turtle tracks and not another soul.

Your own island for the day

Wassaw and Ossabaw are barrier islands with no bridge, no cars and miles of empty beach. We run you out through the sound, get you ashore with your cooler and chairs, and come back for you whenever you say. In between: shelling, wading, sea turtle tracks in season, and the kind of quiet you cannot find anywhere with a road.
