Questions, answered

Anything not covered here, call or text (912) 800-6418. A real person answers.

What does a trip cost?

$425 for an hour and a half on the water. A half day is $800 and a full day is $1,500. Fuel, the boat and the captain are included on every trip.

Is fuel included?

Yes. Fuel is included on every trip listed here.

How do I pay?

A $100 deposit holds your date. Pay the rest when you book if you like, or settle up at the dock by card or cash.

What if I need to cancel?

Change, cancel or modify up to 3 days before your trip at no cost. Inside 3 days we keep the $100 deposit and refund anything else you have paid, or you can move your date once.

What about weather?

The captain decides whether it is safe to go. If he cancels for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.

Where do we meet?

Delegal Creek Marina on Skidaway Island. We can also pick you up at Isle of Hope, downtown Savannah or Tybee; just tell us when you book.

I do not live at The Landings. How do I get through the gate?

We arrange a gate pass for you. When you book, give us the full name of whoever is driving, and the pass will be waiting at the gate.

What should we bring?

Hats, sunglasses and sun shirts, plus whatever you want to eat and drink. There is a table aboard. We send a short packing note with your confirmation.

Is there a bathroom?

Yes. The boat has a private head.

Can you take us to Wassaw or Ossabaw for the day?

Yes. The Island Beach Day drops you off and comes back for you whenever you say; you pay for the boat time, not the hours on the beach.

Can we go to Daufuskie or Hilton Head?

Yes. Both are full-day charters, and we plan the day with you when you book.

Are kids welcome? Pets?

Kids are welcome. Tell us their ages when you book so the right life jackets are aboard. No pets, please.

How early or late can we go?

The first departure is 7 a.m., and early risers can ask about a sunrise trip. Sunset trips are timed so we are tied up again before dark.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Yes. Bring your own food and drinks. There is a table aboard, so a picnic or an early dinner on the water works well.
