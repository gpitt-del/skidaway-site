Questions, answered

Anything not covered here, call or text (912) 800-6418. A real person answers.

What does a trip cost?

$425 for the first hour and a half, for the whole boat, not per person. After that, $125 for each half hour. There is no maximum.

Is fuel included?

Yes. Fuel is included on every trip listed here.

How do I pay?

A $100 deposit holds your date. You can pay the rest when you book if you like, or settle up at the dock by card or cash.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 30 or more days before your trip and the deposit comes back to you. Inside 30 days the deposit is not refundable.

What about weather?

The captain decides whether it is safe to go. If he cancels for weather, you choose a full refund or a new date.

Where do we meet?

Delegal Creek Marina on Skidaway Island. We can also pick you up at Isle of Hope, downtown Savannah, Hutchinson Island or Tybee for an added $150.

I do not live at The Landings. How do I get through the gate?

We arrange a gate pass for you. When you book, give us the full name of whoever is driving, and the pass will be waiting at the gate.

What should we bring?

Hats, sunglasses and sun shirts do most of the work. Mineral sunscreen lotion is welcome. Please leave spray and aerosol sunscreen at home; it ruins the upholstery.

Is there a bathroom?

Yes. The boat has a private head and a private cabin.

Can you take us to Wassaw or Ossabaw for the day?

Yes. The island shuttle is $425 round trip to Wassaw, Ossabaw, or Fish Tales in Richmond Hill. We drop you off and come back for you.

Can we go to Daufuskie?

Yes. It is a longer run, so Daufuskie is a charter of at least five hours rather than a shuttle.

We have a bigger group. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us about your group and what you have in mind, and we will put something together.

Do you run fishing trips?

Offshore fishing is something we plan with you directly rather than book online. Call or text and we will talk through the day.

Are kids welcome? Pets?

Kids are welcome. Tell us their ages when you book so the right life jackets are aboard. No pets, please.

How early or late can we go?

The first departure is 7 a.m., and early risers can ask about a sunrise trip. Sunset trips are timed so we are tied up again before dark.

Can we bring drinks?

Yes. Bring your own drinks and snacks.
